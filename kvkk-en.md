## 2. Personal data processed

**Special category personal data (KVKK Art. 6):**
- Health data: period dates, cycle lengths, flow intensity, physical and
  emotional symptoms, basal body temperature, weight, ovulation test
  results, free-text health notes
- Data concerning sex life: sexual activity logs, contraception
  information

**Other personal data:**
- Transaction security data: a random device identifier (UUID) generated
  by the app
- App preferences: language, units, notification times

**Data we do not collect:** first name, last name, email, phone, national
ID number, location, advertising identifier.

## 3. Purposes of processing

- Providing menstrual cycle prediction and tracking
- Storing symptom logs and analysing patterns
- Providing AI-assisted commentary and chat
- Sending notifications and reminders
- Exchanging data with the Apple Health app
- Managing subscriptions
- Preventing misuse of the service

## 4. Legal basis

**Special category personal data** is processed only **with your explicit
consent under KVKK Art. 6.**

Other data:
- Subscription management: KVKK Art. 5/2-c (performance of a contract)
- Misuse prevention: KVKK Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest)

## 5. Method of collection

You enter the data directly in the app. If you give permission, it can
also be read from the Apple Health app. No personal data is collected
automatically in the background.

## 6. Transfers

**Domestic transfers:** None. Your cycle and symptom data is not
transferred to our servers; it stays on your device.

**International transfers:** Only in the following cases and only with
your separate explicit consent:

| Recipient | Data transferred | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) | AI request content (proxy server) | Request routing, misuse prevention |
| Google LLC (USA) | Cycle lengths, phase, symptom tags, language | Generating one-off comments (Gemini) |
| Anthropic, PBC (USA) | Cycle lengths, phase, symptom tags, chat messages you type, language | Generating chat replies (Claude) |
| RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) | Anonymous user ID, subscription status | Subscription verification |

These transfers rely on your **explicit consent** under KVKK Art. 9. If
you do not consent, the app keeps working on-device and rule-based; none
of the core features are disabled.

## 7. Retention period

Your data is stored on your device until you delete it. When you delete
the app, the data on the device is deleted too. We do not retain data
sent to the AI provider.

## 8. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

Regarding your personal data, you have the right to:

a) Learn whether it is processed
b) Request information if it has been processed
c) Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly
d) Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred
e) Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate
f) Request its erasure or destruction
g) Request that the actions under (e) and (f) be notified to third
   parties to whom it was transferred
h) Object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by
   automated systems
i) Claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing

Because most of your data lives only on your device, you can exercise
some of these rights directly in the app: Settings → Export data /
Delete all data / AI consent.

For other requests, write to atabeksoft+privacy@gmail.com. Your request
will be concluded within 30 days at the latest.
